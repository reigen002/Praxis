## Suggested synthetic founder priorities

Every example below is invented for product demonstration. None is a reported YFS incident.

1. **An upcoming fictional cohort needs a readiness decision.** A dated programme record shows outstanding mentor confirmations or host project briefs; propose an owner and a deadline before the kickoff.
2. **A school needs the next step after a partnership call.** Combine the meeting note, proposed delivery date, coordinator response, and open action into a sourced follow-up draft.
3. **Aggregate student onboarding is incomplete.** Reconcile accepted, confirmed, and ready counts, identify the responsible coordinator, and escalate only the unresolved launch dependency. Use aggregate counts rather than real student records.
4. **A mentor commitment has changed.** Show the affected placement, backup availability, and scope of the decision needed; prepare a draft for human review.
5. **A sponsor update is due but outcomes are unverified.** Flag missing completion or attendance evidence, assign reconciliation, and keep pledged funding distinct from received funding.
6. **A partner renewal needs preparation.** Gather the fictional agreement, delivered activities, verified outcomes, and next meeting into a concise brief with unresolved questions.
7. **A newsletter is ready for review.** Keep it visible as routine marketing work; prioritize time-sensitive programme commitments using the records' actual dates and consequences.

Useful demonstration measures include confirmed mentors versus required placements, ready participants versus accepted participants, complete project briefs versus planned placements, overdue partner actions, and independently verified programme outcomes. These are proposed measures, not claimed YFS KPIs.
